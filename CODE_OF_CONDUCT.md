# Code of Conduct

This covers both the codebase and the WeCos community — the platform, Coffee
Clubs and events.

## The standard

WeCos exists so founders can build calmly. That is the bar for behaviour too.

**Expected**

- Assume good faith. Most disagreements are missing context, not bad intent.
- Critique the work, not the person. "This query will not scale" is useful;
  "this is amateur" is not.
- Be specific. Vague negativity costs the recipient time and teaches nothing.
- Respect that founders here are at wildly different stages. Somebody's obvious
  is somebody else's breakthrough.
- Keep what is shared in confidence, in confidence. Founders discuss revenue,
  failures and funding here.

**Not acceptable**

- Harassment, discrimination or personal attacks of any kind
- Sexual language or imagery, and unwelcome attention
- Publishing private information without permission
- Using the network primarily to spam, scrape or cold-sell
- Misrepresenting your company, traction, funding or credentials
- Retaliation against anyone who reports a problem

## Reporting

Email **start@wecos.in**. Reports are handled confidentially. Include what
happened, where, and any evidence you have.

We will acknowledge within two working days and tell you the outcome.

## Enforcement

Depending on severity: a private warning, temporary suspension, removal from
community spaces, or termination of membership. Membership fees are not
automatically refunded on termination for cause.

Decisions are made by the WeCos team. If you believe one was wrong, reply and ask
for a review; a different person will look at it.
